# Basic chat AI users in Barcelona

*Desk research, September 2026. Claude Impact Lab.*

**Scope:** people who use chat assistants (ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Claude) for simple questions and answers, not advanced or technical use.

**Main caveat:** there is no published data on generative AI use for the city of Barcelona itself. The figures below are for Catalonia and Spain, with global data where marked. Barcelona is probably above the Catalan average: it is a young, highly educated city, 35.4% of residents were born abroad, and it has more than 135,000 ICT workers. That is an inference, not a measured figure.

| Figure | Value | Area and source |

**Who uses it (Spain 2025, INE):**

- **Age:** 75.6% of 16–24s and 59.3% of 25–34s. Use drops steeply after 45.
- **Education:** 50.6% of people with high education. In Catalonia, 64% of people with higher education use it (CEO).
- **Work:** 50.8% of office and other non-manual workers.
- **Place:** 41% in cities.
- **Gender:** the gap is small (men 39.9%, women 35.9%) and nearly closed among younger people.
- **Purpose:** mostly personal (30.2%), then work (17.9%) and study (16.2%).

## 2. How basic users behave

- **Personal use comes first:** 73% of ChatGPT messages worldwide are not about work. The main uses are practical advice, looking things up, and writing or editing their own text (OpenAI/NBER 2025).
- **Advice questions:** about half of Catalans ask AI about health, legal or money matters at least occasionally (CEO).
- **Translation is a standout use:** it is the top reason for using AI for news in Spain (33%), and 51% of workers use AI translation tools. This matters in a city that mixes Catalan, Spanish and English.
- **Shadow AI at work:**
  - 63% of Spanish workers use AI at work (InfoJobs 2026).
  - 77% of knowledge workers who use it bring their own tools (Microsoft, 2024).
  - Only 22% see a clear AI strategy at their company.
- **Students:** 89% of university students use it, 35% daily. Only 34% have had any training (Fundación CYD).
- **Trust vs checking:**
  - Trust is low: only 18% trust chatbot news answers.
  - In surveys 65% say they check answers, yet only 38% click through to sources.
  - 86% of workers treat AI output as a draft they check themselves.
- **Habits:** people stick with one free tool and use it like a search engine. Use is probably mostly on mobile, but no data on devices was found.
- **Where Claude fits:** in Spain, Claude is used mostly by technical people (Anthropic Economic Index), so basic users mainly use ChatGPT and Gemini.

**Possible additions:** freelancers and small-business owners, and tourists planning trips. No Spain-specific data was found for either.

## 4. Uncertain points

- **Catalonia's 44.9%:** not yet checked against INE's own tables.
- **Survey dates:** sources give different fieldwork dates for the CEO barometer (Jan–Feb vs May–Jun 2026). The Funcas survey is dated December 2024 or December 2025.
- **ONTSI 2026 figures:** taken from a search summary; the report itself couldn't be opened.
- **Not found:** data on devices, income, or Barcelona districts.
- **Not comparable:** survey figures and passive browsing data (GfK 62% reach) measure different things.
