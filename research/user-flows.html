Clawd Flow Map

Every way a user can move through the Clawd coach, from first visit to coming back a week later. Built from the pitch doc, the prototype and Frank's MVP plan. Hero persona: Carmen, 52, whose office expects AI but gives no training.

Prototype built and clickable now MVP in Frank's build plan Later pitch only or after the hackathon

The whole map

Three ways in, one core loop, and the orchestrator in the middle deciding whether Clawd speaks. Everything Clawd teaches ends up in the profile, which makes the next answer better. That return arrow is the product.

The flows

Each flow on its own, with what triggers it, what Clawd does and how far along it is. Open "Experience and edge cases" for the detail.

Flow 1

Onboarding

Prototype

Trigger: first visit, no account, nothing known about the user.

flowchart TD
  A["Lands on the page"] --> B["Clawd, big and centred:
what should I call you?"]
  B -->|Skip the intro| Z["Chat on
Only when useful"]
  B --> C["What brings you here?"]
  C --> D["How much help?
level suggested from answer"]
  D --> E["Flag wave, walks
to the message box"]
  E --> F["Greeting with name
+ 3 suggestions"]
Experience and edge cases
  • Three questions maximum. Role, tone and rules are asked later, when Clawd can show their effect.
  • "I have something to get done" suggests Only when useful. "I want to learn" suggests Guide me and opens the Lab list.
  • Open: Frank's plan also asks language here. Worth adding, since the first answer depends on it.
Flow 2

Real task with coaching

Prototype

Trigger: Carmen types a one-line request. This is the hero demo.

flowchart TD
  A["One-line request:
translate to Catalan"] --> B["Claude answers
with no context"]
  B --> C{"Generic answer?
budget left?"}
  C -->|no| D["Clawd stays idle"]
  C -->|yes| E["Clawd hops:
teach me how you write?"]
  E -->|Not now| F["Gap doubles"]
  E -->|Never| G["Tip type blocked"]
  E -->|Teach me| H["Lab mission
Flow 3"]
  H --> I["Same request again"]
  I --> J["Better answer
+ notes for you"]
  J --> K["Compare before
and after"]
Experience and edge cases
  • The task always comes first. Clawd speaks only after the answer has finished and Carmen pauses.
  • If Claude is unavailable, the prototype shows labelled example answers so the demo still works.
  • Risk: Claude's "before" can already be decent. The visible gain is tone, ready-to-paste text and the flagged ambiguity.
Flow 3

Lab mission

Prototype

Trigger: accepts a nudge, taps a mission, or taps "Try it in the Lab" on an explanation.

flowchart TD
  A["Opens the Lab
separate conversation"] --> B["Who are you
with clients?"]
  A -->|Leave the Lab| X["Back to chat
nothing saved"]
  B --> C["How should
emails sound?"]
  C --> D["What must
never go wrong?"]
  D --> E["Save"]
  E --> F["Memory + skill saved
confetti"]
  F --> G["Try it on
the real task"]
Experience and edge cases
  • Clawd lifts weights while Carmen is in the Lab, and throws confetti when she saves.
  • A separate conversation keeps practice out of her real chat, so Lab examples never leak into the client email.
  • Open: in Frank's plan this reward is a collectible card. The Lab mission can award the card, so both ideas fit.
Flow 4

Remembering with consent

MVP

Trigger: a personal detail comes up in chat, or Clawd spots a repeated preference.

flowchart TD
  A["Detail comes up:
it's for my mother"] --> B{"Sensitive?
ID, diagnosis, amount"}
  B -->|yes| C["Keep a general version
or nothing"]
  B -->|no| D["Clawd: should
I remember that?"]
  D -->|Yes| E["Saved to profile"]
  D -->|No| F["Discarded"]
  E --> G["What Clawd remembers:
see, edit, delete"]
  G --> H["Export my AI profile
for any assistant"]
Experience and edge cases
  • Nothing is ever saved silently. Each "should I remember?" is itself the lesson for the Remember me card.
  • Without an account the profile lives in one browser. Export is the safety net.
  • Clawd occasionally says what he used ("kept it formal, like you asked") so Carmen links the profile to better answers.
Flow 5

Explain on demand

Prototype

Trigger: Guide me level. Carmen hovers or taps anything she doesn't understand.

flowchart TD
  A["Guide me level"] --> B["Orange dots on
confusing UI"]
  B --> C["Hover or tap"]
  C --> D["One-line explanation
in plain words"]
  D --> E{"Wants more?"}
  E -->|yes| F["Try it in the Lab"]
  E -->|no| G["Back to work"]
Experience and edge cases
  • User-started, so it never counts against the interruption budget.
  • Covers the side nav (Projects, Artifacts, Scheduled, Design, Customize) and in-chat labels like "Memory updated".
  • Phones have no hover, so the dots are tap targets there.
Flow 6

Clawd learns to back off

Prototype

Trigger: any suggestion Clawd starts, and any change of help level.

flowchart TD
  A["Clawd suggestion"] --> B{"Carmen's response"}
  B -->|Accept| C["Next tip may
come sooner"]
  B -->|Not now| D["Gap for that tip
type doubles"]
  D --> E{"2 dismissals
this session?"}
  E -->|yes| F["Offer: Only
when useful"]
  B -->|Never| G["That tip type
blocked for good"]
  H["Level set to Off"] --> I["Clawd leaves
the canvas"]
  I --> J["Clawd in the side nav
brings him back"]
Experience and edge cases
  • The prototype has the step-down offer and Off. The doubling gap and per-type blocks are orchestrator rules for the MVP.
  • Budget per level, to test: Guide me 1 every 3 turns and 3 per session; Only when useful 1 per session; Off none.
Flow 7

Safety moments

MVP

Trigger: a health, legal or money topic, or personal data pasted into chat.

flowchart TD
  A["Health, legal, money
or personal data"] --> B{"Help level"}
  B -->|Guide me or
Only when useful| C["Clawd bubble,
skips the budget"]
  B -->|Off| D["Claude adds the note
inside its answer"]
  C --> E["Card: Check
before trusting"]
Experience and edge cases
  • This is the main impact story in Frank's plan: 65% say they check answers, only 38% click through to sources.
  • Off stays truly quiet from Clawd, but the safety content still reaches the user through Claude.
Flow 8

Missions and coming back

MVP

Trigger: end of a session, then a return visit with the same browser.

flowchart TD
  A["End of session"] --> B["Mission: try this on
your next client email"]
  B --> C["Days later:
Carmen returns"]
  C --> D["Clawd: hi Carmen,
how did it go?"]
  D --> E{"Did she try it?"}
  E -->|yes| F["Card unlocked
celebrate"]
  E -->|not yet| G["No guilt,

Experience and edge cases
  • Anti-Duolingo rule: Clawd is happy when she comes back and never sad when she doesn't.
  • Demo shortcut: a "one week later" button reloads with the same profile.

Open questions for the team

  • Language in onboarding? Frank's plan asks it up front, the prototype doesn't. I'd add it as the only fourth question, since every answer depends on it.
  • Cards or Lab missions as the reward? Both fit if a finished mission awards a card. Decide what the demo shows.
  • Should Clawd stay visible on Only when useful? Today he disappears until he has a suggestion, so a new user may not know he exists.
  • Are the budget numbers right? They are starting guesses. Test with 3 to 5 people over 45 before pitch day.
  • Where does the Lab live on phones? The bottom sheet works, but a full-screen Lab may feel more like a game.
